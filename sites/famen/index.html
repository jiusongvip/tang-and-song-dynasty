Famen Temple Museum

Famen Si · Famen Si

Built: 874 Dynasty: Tang Dynasty Location: Baoji, Shaanxi Admission: 120 CNY Time needed: 2-3 hrs

Honest note: The above-ground temple is modern. Only the underground crypt and museum collection are Tang.

Basis: State Council protected site — click Authenticity Labels to understand our classification system.

Why Visit

1987 discovery of an intact Tang underground palace sealed in 874. Contained Buddha finger-bone relic plus 2,499 items of Tang gold, silver, glass, and textiles. Greatest Tang archaeological find.

How to Get There

Famen Temple (法门寺) is 120km west of Xi'an in Fufeng County, Baoji City. From Xi'an: take HSR from Xi'an North Station (西安北站) to Baoji South Station (宝鸡南站) -- 1 hour, 60 CNY, frequent departures every 30 min. From Baoji South, taxi 30 min to Famen Temple (40 CNY). Alternative: direct bus from Xi'an West Bus Station (城西客运站) to Famen Temple -- 2.5 hours, 35 CNY, departures at 8am and 9am daily. Private car from Xi'an: 2-2.5 hours via G30 expressway, 400 CNY return with driver waiting. The temple is well-signposted from the expressway exit. Day trip from Xi'an is very manageable -- leave at 7am, return by 4pm.

Where to Stay

Most visitors day-trip from Xi'an -- no need to stay overnight near the temple. Baoji city (25km from Famen): Crowne Plaza Baoji (400-500 CNY/night, best hotel in Baoji, English-speaking staff); Baoji Marriott (350-450 CNY/night). Famen town: Famen Temple Hotel (法门寺酒店, 200 CNY/night, basic but walking distance to temple, hot water, Chinese only). Best strategy: stay in Xi'an, day-trip to Famen. If you want to explore more of Baoji area (Bronze Ware Museum, Taibai Mountain), stay 1-2 nights in Baoji.

What to Eat

Baoji cuisine is classic Shaanxi with its own twist. Must-try: saozi mian (臊子面, minced pork and vegetable noodle soup, the Qishan county specialty, 15-20 CNY); gan mian pi (擀面皮, cold flat noodles with chili oil, the quintessential Baoji street food, 8-12 CNY); rou jia mo (腊汁肉夹馍, Fufeng style is slightly sweeter than Xi'an, 10-15 CNY). The Famen Temple complex has a Buddhist vegetarian restaurant (40 CNY/person, buffet style, attached to the temple). The food is simple but authentic -- monks eat here. In Baoji city: Lao Cheng Yi Wan Mian (老城一碗面) near the train station serves Qishan saozi mian and other local noodles, 30-50 CNY/person.

Best Time to Visit

April, May, September, October: best weather, 15-25C. The museum is indoor and climate-controlled so the experience is good year-round. Summer (June-August): 30-38C outside but the underground museum and galleries are air-conditioned. Winter (November-February): 0-10C, much fewer visitors, atmospheric with winter light on the pagoda. Hours: 8:30am-5:30pm (last entry 4:30pm). Arrive at opening (8:30am) -- the underground crypt has a timed entry system and morning groups are smaller. Chinese New Year: temple holds a large prayer festival, very crowded but culturally interesting. Avoid Golden Week (Oct 1-7): domestic tourist crush.

Tips for Foreign Travelers

CRITICAL: The above-ground temple complex is a massive, modern, and not-subtle construction built in the 2000s. It is architecturally questionable. Ignore it. What matters: (1) The underground museum -- this is where the Tang crypt was discovered in 1987. You walk through the actual excavation site. (2) The Treasure Gallery -- houses the Tang gold, silver, glass, and textile artifacts. The four nested Buddhist reliquary sets (gold-silver-gold-stone caskets containing the Buddha finger bone sarira) are displayed here. The famous four-faced Buddha staff (四股十二环锡杖) is a highlight. English labels are minimal in most galleries -- download the Smart Famen app or hire an English-speaking guide at the museum entrance (200 CNY, 1.5 hours, worth it). Photography is permitted in the museum (no flash). The sacred finger bone relic itself is displayed in a special chapel with controlled lighting -- no photos in this room.

Poetry Landmarks: Walk Where Tang and Song Poets Walked

Tang poetry and Song ci lyrics are the heart of Chinese literature. Li Bai drank under the moon. Du Fu watched his country fall. Su Shi wrote about pork belly and moonlight on the river. You can visit the places they immortalized.

Mount Emei (峨眉山) — Li Bai's Moon

Li Bai's most famous short poem begins: "The moon above Mount Emei / half a wheel of autumn." The mountain was a Tang-era Buddhist center and a place of pilgrimage. Today it's a UNESCO site and one of China's Four Sacred Buddhist Mountains. The Golden Summit (3077m) has a Tang-dynasty bronze temple.

Visit: Sichuan province. HSR from Chengdu to Emeishan (1.5h, 65 CNY). Cable car to summit. Budget 2 days.

Read: "The Moon over Mount Emei" (峨眉山月歌) by Li Bai

Yellow Crane Tower (黄鹤楼) — Li Bai's Silence

The tower in Wuhan was a Tang-era landmark where poets gathered. Cui Hao's poem about it was so perfect that Li Bai, visiting years later, wrote: "I would write, but Cui Hao has already said it all." The current tower is a 1985 reconstruction — but the view of the Yangtze is real.

Visit: Wuhan, Hubei. Metro Line 5 to Yellow Crane Tower Station. Admission: 70 CNY. Best at sunset.

West Lake (西湖) — Su Shi's Masterpiece

Su Shi (Su Dongpo) governed Hangzhou twice and transformed West Lake. He dredged it, built the Su Causeway (苏堤) lined with willow and peach trees, and wrote its most famous poem: "Compare West Lake to Xi Shi, the beauty / light makeup or heavy, always just right."

Visit: Hangzhou. Walk the Su Causeway at dawn for the "Spring Dawn on Su Causeway" view — one of the Ten Scenes of West Lake. Free.

Du Fu's Thatched Cottage (杜甫草堂) — Chengdu

Du Fu lived here from 760–765 after fleeing the An Lushan Rebellion. In this modest cottage he wrote over 240 poems — some of the greatest in Chinese literature. "My thatched roof torn by autumn wind" was written here. The current site is a Ming-Qing reconstruction with a beautiful bamboo garden.

Visit: Chengdu, Sichuan. Metro Line 4 to Caotang North Road. Admission: 60 CNY. Budget 2 hours. The garden is as rewarding as the cottage.

Red Cliffs (赤壁) — Su Shi's Imagination

su wrote his two "Odes to the Red Cliffs" about a boat trip on the Yangtze near Huangzhou (now Huanggang, Hubei). He was actually at the WRONG Red Cliffs — the historical battle site is 200km upstream. But Su Shi's poems made HIS cliffs the famous ones.

Visit: Huanggang, Hubei. HSR from Wuhan (30 min). "Su Shi's Red Cliffs" park: 40 CNY.

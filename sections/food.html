Tang and Song Food: What to Eat and Where

China's two greatest dynasties also shaped what Chinese people eat today. Tang brought Silk Road ingredients to the table. Song invented the restaurant as we know it. You can still taste both in the cities they once ruled.

Tang Dynasty Food (618–907)

Tang Chang'an was the most cosmopolitan city on earth. Persian merchants, Central Asian traders, and Indian monks all brought their food. Wheat became dominant in the north. The elite drank grape wine, ate lamb, and flavored everything with freshly ground pepper from India.

Hu Food (胡食) — Silk Road Cuisine

Hu bing (胡饼, sesame flatbread from Central Asia) was the Tang equivalent of pizza — sold on every street corner. Bing (pastries and flatbreads) were invented by Turkic and Persian bakers and became Chang'an's street food staple.

Grape Wine and Foreign Drinks

The Tang elite abandoned traditional grain alcohol for grape wine imported from the Western Regions. Tang poets wrote endlessly about wine. Li Bai's most famous poems were composed drunk. Kumiss (fermented mare's milk) was also popular among nobles.

Tea Emerges

Tea drinking spread from Buddhist monasteries to the general population during Tang. Lu Yu wrote The Classic of Tea (《茶经》) around 760, the world's first comprehensive book on tea. By the late Tang, tea was a national obsession.

Song Dynasty Food (960–1279)

The Song invented the restaurant. Before Song, you ate at home or at inns for travelers. The Song commercial revolution created specialized eateries in Kaifeng and Hangzhou — noodle shops, dumpling houses, Sichuan pepper restaurants, vegetarian Buddhist eateries. Street food culture exploded.

The World's First Restaurants

Kaifeng had restaurants open 24 hours, serving hundreds of dishes. Menus existed. Dongjing Meng Hua Lu (《东京梦华录》, "Dreams of Splendor of the Eastern Capital") documents 300+ named dishes available in 12th-century Kaifeng.

Rice Takes Over

New fast-ripening Champa rice from Vietnam doubled yields. Rice became the southern staple. Meanwhile, wheat noodle culture exploded in the north. Mian (noodles) and jiaozi (dumplings) became what they are today.

Dian Cha (点茶) — Whisked Tea Art

The Song elite whisked powdered tea into foam using bamboo whisks, then competed to create the most beautiful froth. This art was later adopted by Buddhist monasteries and spread throughout East Asia. Emperor Huizong wrote a treatise on tea.

Where to Eat Tang and Song Food Today

Xi'an — Taste Tang Dynasty Chang'an

Yang rou pao mo (lamb soup with torn bread, 羊肉泡馍, 30–50 CNY) is Tang-era military food. Rou jia mo (肉夹馍, 10–15 CNY) descends from hu bing. The Muslim Quarter (回民街) carries Tang Silk Road food traditions: lamb skewers, persimmon cakes, pomegranate juice. Defachang Dumpling House near Bell Tower has served dumplings since 1936 — try the dumpling banquet (100–200 CNY).

Kaifeng — Eat Like a Song Emperor

Guan tang bao (灌汤包, soup dumplings, 40–60 CNY per basket at Diyilou, open since 1922) are Kaifeng's defining dish. Tong zi ji (桶子鸡, braised young chicken) and chao liang fen (炒凉粉, fried mung bean jelly) are Song-era street foods still sold at the Drum Tower Night Market. For the full experience, eat at Dongjing Restaurant inside Qingming Riverside Garden for dishes recreated from Song cookbooks.

Hangzhou — Southern Song Capital Flavors

Dongpo rou (东坡肉, braised pork belly, 60–80 CNY) is named after Su Shi (Su Dongpo), the Song poet-official who invented (or popularized) it. Longjing shrimp (龙井虾仁, shrimp stir-fried with Dragon Well tea leaves) and West Lake fish in vinegar sauce (西湖醋鱼) are classic Hangzhou dishes. Louwailou Restaurant (楼外楼, since 1848) on West Lake serves all three.

Suzhou — Jiangnan Refinement

Songshu guiyu (松鼠桂鱼, squirrel-shaped mandarin fish, 120–180 CNY) is the most famous Suzhou dish. Xie fen xiaolong (蟹粉小笼, crab roe soup dumplings) are seasonal, best in autumn. Song He Lou (松鹤楼, since 1757) on Shantang Street serves classic Suzhou banquet cuisine. For street food: sheng jian bao (生煎包, pan-fried pork buns) along Shantang Street.
